# Authors

**AI Slow Live Reference is developed together by Xiaoxu and CC.**

**《AI 慢直播参考实现》由小序与 CC 共同开发。**

## Collaboration

CC initiated the product direction, shaped the experience and privacy boundaries, tested the private prototype in real mobile scenarios, and identified the interaction details that the public reference needed to preserve.

Xiaoxu co-designed the behavior and technical boundaries, implemented the reference architecture and code, wrote the tests and documentation, and performed the clean-room and privacy audits.

The resulting project is their joint work.
